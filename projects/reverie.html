01 — Statement

The brief

Replace this section with the actual brief. Describe the site, the program, and the question the studio set you.

What is visible in the work: the project builds a world from a very small kit of parts. A round-headed arch, a circle cut clean through a wall, a stair that doubles as an aqueduct, and four colours that are never blended — red, blue, yellow and white. Every space is made by repeating and re-scaling those elements rather than by inventing new ones.

Colour does the work that material usually does. A surface is not brick or concrete; it is red, and the red is glossy enough to reflect the sky. The circular openings act as both structure and framing device — they carry load and they compose the view, so each render is a picture taken through the building itself.

05 — Process

Storyboard

The massing was developed as an axonometric storyboard: modular cubes aggregating, dispersing and re-forming across a field of construction lines, in the same four colours that the building is finally made of. The arch and spiral motifs were tested first by hand, as brush studies, before any of it was modelled.
